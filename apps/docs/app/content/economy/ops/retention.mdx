An append-only ledger accumulates history for the life of the economy, and the
[archive and retention sweeps](/economy/ops/archival/) exist so the hot store does not
carry all of it forever. That creates one contract every consumer of history must know.

## What the live store still answers, and what it does not

- **The live store serves a window, not an archive.** Statements, postings, and lineage
  reads answer from hot storage. Once the archive sweep has moved a sealed prefix to the
  cold store, those rows are no longer served by the live reads — by design, with the
  pruned edge still provable.
- **Replay is the export plus the archive sink.** The complete, verifiable record is the
  canonical ledger export (`economy.read.export()`, `economy db backup`) composed with the
  pages in the host's `ArchiveSink`. The export carries the signed archive boundary, so
  offline verification proves the hot chain back to the boundary and the boundary's
  signature vouches for everything that moved.
- **Secondary rows have horizons.** Idempotency rows and settled sessions are pruned past
  their configured ages (`idempotencyOlderThanMs`, `sessionsOlderThanMs`) — a settled
  session only after its settle posting re-proves. A replay of a years-old idempotency key
  is answered by the ledger's own dedup, not by the pruned row.
- **No route promises unbounded history.** A public promise of full history over the live
  store would freeze retention into a forever contract; the lab refuses to make it, and
  any page or client built on the reads must not imply it.

## What this means for integrators

Build dashboards and statements on the live reads; build audits and compliance archives on
the export plus the sink. When a consumer needs both, the
[offline verification page](/economy/reference/offline-verification/) shows how the two
compose into one proof.
