This is a [Tier 3](/economy/ops/the-supervisor/) signature. Retry budgets are running out. These submits failed to their
callers after every retry. Unlike a [deadlock-storm](/economy/ops/runbooks/deadlock-storm/),
users are seeing errors, not just latency.

## Symptoms

Callers see CONFLICT failures. `engine.retry.exhausted` warn logs appear. The `engine.retry`
count moves with `outcome: 'exhausted'`.

## Detection

The detector sums `engine.retry` tagged `outcome: 'exhausted'` over
`retryExhaustionWindowMs` (default 5m). A sum that reaches `retryExhaustionThreshold`
(default 5) is a finding. The finding carries `byEngine`, so a mysql-only exhaustion (its
lock ordering differs) is visible at a glance.

## Automatic response

The supervisor emits `detected` + `escalated` once per window. There is no lever. An
exhausted budget means the conflict rate is beyond what retrying absorbs. Retrying harder is
not a remediation.

## Manual steps

1. Confirm the conflict source exactly as in
   [deadlock-storm](/economy/ops/runbooks/deadlock-storm/) — the engine deadlock counters,
   sampled through the burst.
2. Compare `engine.retry.recovered` against `engine.retry`: a healthy budget rescues most
   conflicts. High pressure with low recovery is systematic lock-order contention, not bad
   luck.
3. Reduce contention at the source: lower submit concurrency against the hot accounts, or
   shard the platform accounts. Raising the retry budget converts errors back into latency —
   sometimes the right call during an incident, never the fix.

## Escalation

Escalate with the `byEngine` breakdown when exhaustion is sustained across windows. At that
point you are in a capacity and design conversation.
