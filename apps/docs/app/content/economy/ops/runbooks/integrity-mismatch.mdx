This is a [Tier 3](/economy/ops/the-supervisor/) signature, escalate-never-fix, plus containment. The scheduled checkpoint
reverify says the ledger no longer matches what was sealed. Rows changed behind the hash
chain.

## Symptoms

`worker.checkpoint.verify` counts arrive with `outcome: 'mismatch'`.
`worker.checkpoint.mismatch` error logs appear. Balances may still look plausible. Tampering
is designed to look plausible.

## Detection

The detector watches for any `worker.checkpoint.verify` meter signal tagged
`outcome: 'mismatch'`, or the `worker.checkpoint.mismatch` log event, past a high-water mark.
Each signal fires exactly one episode, ever.

## Automatic response

The integrity pass runs first in every tick, before the saga poll, so a hung store cannot
block it. On a finding, the supervisor does three things:

1. It emits `detected`, then runs one read-only `prove()` — the only unscheduled full prove in
   the system. It then emits `escalated` with the report attached: the drifted accounts and
   the conservation verdict.
2. The **containment latch** sets. Every tier-1 lever (saga sweep, relay re-drive, inbox
   revive) is refused as `suppressed` until the supervisor restarts.
3. If the host wired `pauseWorker`, the scheduled loop pauses and the supervisor emits
   `acted`. No new checkpoint then seals over the evidence. Resuming the loop is a human
   decision; the supervisor will not make it.

Nothing is ever auto-fixed — the mismatched rows are the evidence you investigate with.

## Manual steps

1. Read the escalation's attached prove report: which accounts drifted, and by how much
   (`materialized` vs `derived` per account).
2. Freeze operationally: keep the worker paused; stop deploys against the store.
3. `make prove` re-runs the full prover on demand while you investigate.
4. Compare against the last good backup ([the drill](/economy/ops/backup-restore/) proves what
   a dump contains) and database audit logs to find when and how the rows changed.
5. Only after the cause is understood: repair the data at the database layer under review,
   `worker.resume()` (or restart the worker process), restart the supervisor to clear the
   latch, and watch the next `worker.checkpoint.verify` beat come back `ok`.

## Escalation

This signature IS the escalation. It means unauthorized writes or corruption in the system of
record. Treat it as a security incident until proven otherwise. A mismatch right after a
signer change is almost always a [key-rotation mistake](/economy/ops/key-rotation/). Rule that
out first.
